Aspiron CBSE: NCERT chapter tests for Class 11 and 12

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Aspiron Education's online tests are NCERT-based and chapter-wise, so CBSE Class 11 and 12 science students can practise Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Maths on an exam-style screen and get an instant result with solutions. Tests are for both English medium and हिंदी medium students: where a series offers both, you pick your medium once and every test opens in it.

How CBSE students use Aspiron

  • Chapter-wise tests after you finish each NCERT chapter in school or coaching.
  • Instant result with solutions, so you see which NCERT topics to read again.
  • One preparation for boards and entrance exams: the same NCERT chapters are the base of NEET and JEE.
  • English and हिंदी medium in series that offer both.

The series that are open right now, and what each one contains, are listed on the Store. For entrance exams see Aspiron NEET and Aspiron JEE.

Frequently asked questions

Does Aspiron Education have tests for CBSE students?

Aspiron's chapter-wise tests are NCERT-based, so CBSE Class 11 and 12 Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Maths students can use them for practice. The Store shows which series are open right now.

Are Aspiron tests based on NCERT?

Yes. Questions are tagged as NCERT based, previous year (PYQ) or extra, and tests follow the Class 11 and 12 chapters of the NCERT books that CBSE uses.

Is Aspiron linked to CBSE?

No. Aspiron Education is an independent platform and is not linked to CBSE, NCERT or NTA. Board names are used only to describe the syllabus.

Can I take the tests in Hindi medium?

Yes, in series marked English + हिंदी medium. You choose your medium once and every test opens in that language.
