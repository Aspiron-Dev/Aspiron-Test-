How to buy

  1. 1
    Tap “Buy now”

    Your name and mobile number are already saved — no need to type them again.

  2. 2
    Pay with UPI

    Scan the QR, or tap “Save QR to pay” and choose it from your gallery in GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any UPI app.

  3. 3
    Tap “I have already paid”

    Write the name on the account you paid from, and the UTR number if you have it.

  4. 4
    Verify on Telegram or WhatsApp

    Send the message with your payment screenshot. We check it and send your login details.

Safe payment

Pay only to the UPI ID shown on this page. Check the name in your UPI app before paying. Every order has its own code so your payment is matched correctly.

Questions

When do I get my test series?

After we check your payment screenshot on Telegram or WhatsApp. We then send your login details there.

Which apps can I pay with?

Any UPI app — Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or your bank's app.

Are the reviews real?

Yes. A review can only be written from a student's own account, after their test series is unlocked. Each student can review a series only once.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It works on any phone or computer with a browser, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

I paid but didn't get a reply

Message us on Telegram or WhatsApp with your order code and screenshot. We reply as soon as possible.
