NEET Test Series: online CBT mock tests

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Aspiron's NEET test series gives you full-length and chapter-wise online mock tests in the NTA pattern (180 questions, 720 marks, +4/−1), with an instant result, all-India rank among Aspiron students and a detailed analysis. Tests work on any phone, in English or हिंदी medium.

What you get in the NEET test series

  • Real exam screen (CBT): timer, question palette, mark for review and section switching, like a computer-based exam.
  • Instant result: score, accuracy, rank among all students who took the test, and a subject-wise and chapter-wise analysis.
  • Solutions for every question, plus a list of your mistakes and saved questions to revise later.
  • Previous year questions (PYQ) and a question bank for chapter-wise practice.
  • English and हिंदी medium: where a series offers both, every student gets the tests in the medium they choose.
  • Works on any phone or computer, and can be installed like an app. Live tests send a phone notification.

How to use the tests well

Take one full test a week in the same 3-hour slot as the real exam, then spend as long again on the analysis. Read the guide how to use NEET mock tests and the NEET exam pattern and marking scheme.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Aspiron NEET test series based on the latest NTA pattern?

Yes. Full-length tests follow the NEET (UG) pattern: 180 questions from Physics, Chemistry, Botany and Zoology, 720 marks, +4 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong one, in 3 hours.

Is the test series available in Hindi medium?

Yes, for series marked English + हिंदी medium. You choose your medium once and every test opens in that language.

Can I take the NEET mock tests on my phone?

Yes. The tests run in the browser on any Android phone, iPhone or computer, and the app can be installed on the home screen. No separate download is needed.

When do I get my result and rank?

Right after you submit, you see your score, accuracy, all-India rank among Aspiron students, and solutions with a subject-wise and chapter-wise analysis.

How do I buy the NEET test series?

Open the Store, pick the series, and pay with UPI. Access is added to your student account after the payment is checked. Details are in the Refund & Delivery policy.
