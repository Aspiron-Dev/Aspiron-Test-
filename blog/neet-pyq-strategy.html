NEET PYQ strategy: using previous year questions

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Solve at least the last 10 years of NEET questions chapter-wise as you finish each chapter, then attempt the last 3–5 papers as full timed tests. PYQ show which NCERT lines and topics are asked again and again, so mark them in your NCERT book.

Why PYQ matter

NEET questions are based on the NCERT syllabus, and many concepts come back in a new form. Previous year questions show the level, the wording and the topics that carry the most marks.

A simple PYQ plan

  • During the syllabus: after each chapter, solve its PYQ from the last 10 years.
  • Mark NCERT: highlight each line a question came from.
  • Last 2–3 months: attempt the most recent papers as full 3-hour tests.
  • Re-solve wrong ones after two weeks without looking at the answer.

Frequently asked questions

How many years of NEET PYQ should I solve?

At least the last 10 years chapter-wise; more if time allows. Solve the most recent 3–5 papers as full timed tests.

Are PYQ enough for NEET?

No. PYQ show the pattern and important topics, but you also need NCERT reading and regular full-length mock tests.
