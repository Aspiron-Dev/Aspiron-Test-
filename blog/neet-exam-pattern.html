NEET exam pattern and marking scheme

Last updated: 13 November 2026

NEET (UG) has 180 compulsory questions (Physics 45, Chemistry 45, Botany 45, Zoology 45) for 720 marks in 3 hours. A correct answer gets +4, a wrong answer −1 and a blank 0. It is a pen-and-paper (OMR) exam conducted by NTA.

This is the NEET (UG) 2025 pattern, when NTA removed the optional Section B and made all 180 questions compulsory. Confirm the pattern for your year in the official bulletin on neet.nta.nic.in.

What the marking means for you

A wrong answer costs 1 mark, so blind guessing adds risk. A guess is worth taking when you can rule out at least one or two options with confidence. Biology is half the paper (360 marks), so it decides most ranks; Physics usually takes the most time per question.

Plan your 180 minutes

  • Biology first (about 60–70 minutes), then Chemistry, then Physics.
  • Do one quick round of every question you are sure of, then return to the rest.
  • Keep the last 10 minutes to check the OMR bubbles.

Practise this plan in a full-length NEET mock test before the exam, not on exam day.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions are there in NEET?

180 questions, all compulsory: 45 each from Physics, Chemistry, Botany and Zoology.

What is the total mark of NEET?

720 marks: 180 questions × 4 marks.

Is there negative marking in NEET?

Yes. Each wrong answer costs 1 mark. Unanswered questions get 0.

Is NEET a computer-based exam?

No. NEET (UG) is a pen-and-paper exam on an OMR sheet. Online mock tests are still useful to build speed and accuracy.
