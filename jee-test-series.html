JEE Main Test Series: online CBT mock tests

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Aspiron's JEE test series gives you computer-based mock tests in the JEE Main pattern (75 questions, 300 marks, +4/−1) on an exam-style screen, with an instant score, rank, solutions and a chapter-wise analysis after every test.

What you get in the JEE test series

  • Real exam screen (CBT): timer, question palette, mark for review and section switching, like a computer-based exam.
  • Instant result: score, accuracy, rank among all students who took the test, and a subject-wise and chapter-wise analysis.
  • Solutions for every question, plus a list of your mistakes and saved questions to revise later.
  • Previous year questions (PYQ) and a question bank for chapter-wise practice.
  • English and हिंदी medium: where a series offers both, every student gets the tests in the medium they choose.
  • Works on any phone or computer, and can be installed like an app. Live tests send a phone notification.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Aspiron JEE test series follow the JEE Main pattern?

Yes. Full-length tests follow JEE Main: 75 questions, 25 each from Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (20 MCQ + 5 numerical), 300 marks, in 3 hours.

Is there negative marking in the JEE mock tests?

Yes, as in JEE Main: +4 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong answer, for both MCQ and numerical questions. Unanswered questions get 0.

Is JEE Main a computer-based test?

Yes. JEE Main is held as a computer-based test (CBT), so practising on an exam-style screen with a timer and question palette helps you manage time.

Can I see solutions after the test?

Yes. After you submit you get your score, rank, solutions for every question and a subject-wise and chapter-wise analysis.

How do I buy the JEE test series?

Open the Store, choose JEE as your course, pick a series and pay with UPI. Access is added to your account after the payment is checked.
