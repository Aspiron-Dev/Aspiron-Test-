Aspiron test series platform: NEET, JEE and CBSE

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The Aspiron test series platform gives online CBT test series for NEET and JEE and NCERT-based chapter tests for CBSE Class 11 and 12, with an instant result, rank and analysis after every test. Tests are for both English medium and हिंदी medium students: where a series offers both, you pick your medium once and every test opens in it.

  • Aspiron NEET test seriesFull-length and chapter-wise mock tests in the NTA pattern: 180 questions, 720 marks.
  • Aspiron JEE Main test seriesComputer-based mock tests in the JEE Main pattern: 75 questions, 300 marks.
  • Aspiron CBSE / NCERT chapter testsNCERT-based chapter-wise practice for Class 11 and 12 science students.
  • Aspiron CBT platformThe exam-style test screen: timer, palette, instant result, rank and analysis.
  • Aspiron CBT test storeHow to see what is inside each series, its price, and buy with UPI.

Every Aspiron test series includes

  • Real exam screen (CBT): timer, question palette, mark for review and section switching, like a computer-based exam.
  • Instant result: score, accuracy, rank among all students who took the test, and a subject-wise and chapter-wise analysis.
  • Solutions for every question, plus a list of your mistakes and saved questions to revise later.
  • Previous year questions (PYQ) and a question bank for chapter-wise practice.
  • English and हिंदी medium: where a series offers both, every student gets the tests in the medium they choose.
  • Works on any phone or computer, and can be installed like an app. Live tests send a phone notification.

Frequently asked questions

Which test series does Aspiron Education offer?

Aspiron offers online CBT test series for NEET (UG) and JEE Main, with NCERT-based chapter tests that CBSE Class 11 and 12 students can also use. The Store lists the series open right now.

Is the Aspiron test series online or offline?

Online. Every test is taken on the Aspiron CBT screen on a phone or computer, and the result comes instantly.

Is the Aspiron test series for Hindi medium students too?

Yes. Aspiron is for both English medium and Hindi medium students; series marked English + हिंदी medium give each student the tests in their own medium.

How do I join an Aspiron test series?

Open the Store, enter your name and mobile number, choose NEET or JEE, pick a series and pay with UPI. The series is then added to your student account.
